Friday is designed to keep you in control of your data and actions.

## Local By Default

The app, settings, provider keys, conversation history, memory, and workspace data live on your machine.

## Bring Your Own Keys

You use your own AI provider accounts. Friday does not put another vendor between you and the providers you choose.

## Explicit Permissions

Actions that write, delete, publish, send messages, access private data, or touch accounts require permission checks. Friday shows confirmation prompts before sensitive work proceeds.

## Hardened App Shell

The interface runs in a sandboxed environment with strict isolation from the underlying system, and browser windows use safe defaults.

## Certification Note

Friday does not currently claim formal regulated-data certification. Review your own AI providers' and connected services' terms for how they handle data you send them.
