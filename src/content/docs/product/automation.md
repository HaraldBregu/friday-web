Friday can work on a schedule even when you are not actively chatting.

## Task Scheduler

The scheduler can create, update, pause, resume, list, delete, and run jobs on demand. Each schedule runs an assistant turn with its own instructions, provider, and model.

Examples:

- Daily summaries.
- Periodic checks.
- Routine cleanup.
- Recurring reminders.

## Health Checks

Health checks run Friday periodically against a checklist you define. You set the interval, provider, model, and checklist instructions that describe what healthy means for your setup.
