# Friday: A Personal Desktop AI Assistant

Friday is a personal desktop AI assistant designed to understand how you work, remember what matters, and help you get things done. It brings a capable AI companion directly to your computer, where it can support your daily life instead of waiting for one-off questions in a browser tab.

The goal of Friday is simple: make everyday life easier by letting a trusted personal assistant handle the repetitive, time-consuming work that gets in the way of more meaningful things.

## An Assistant That Becomes Personal

Friday can grow into an assistant shaped around your routines, preferences, and priorities. With your permission, it can:

- Remember useful context and preferences across conversations.
- Organize schedules, appointments, reminders, and recurring plans.
- Run background and scheduled tasks without requiring constant attention.
- Connect to external services, tools, and information sources.
- Store and retrieve data through connected cloud services when needed.
- Carry out multi-step tasks and return a finished result.

Instead of repeatedly explaining what you need, you can build an ongoing working relationship with an assistant that understands your context. Friday can take care of the boring work, keep track of important details, and help you stay focused on decisions, ideas, and people.

## More Than an Assistant

Friday is not limited to being a general-purpose AI assistant. Its extension system can transform it into specialized software for different professions, interests, and workflows.

Extensions can add focused capabilities for areas such as:

- **Management:** coordinate projects, organize teams, prepare reports, and follow up on work.
- **Design:** support research, creative exploration, asset creation, and design workflows.
- **Architecture:** help develop concepts, organize project information, and assist with technical documentation.
- **Entertainment:** create interactive experiences, manage media, and support games or personal projects.
- **Specialized work:** connect Friday to industry-specific tools, data, and repeatable processes.

This makes Friday a foundation rather than a fixed product. It can begin as a personal desktop assistant and evolve into the software each person needs—personal, extensible, and built around the way they live and work.
